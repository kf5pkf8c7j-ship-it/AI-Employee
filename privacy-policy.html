Privacy Policy

CozySetup.kw · Last updated: 26 September 2026

CozySetup.kw provides camping setups in Kuwait. This policy explains what information we process when you contact us through Instagram Direct Messages, why we process it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Information we process

When you message our Instagram account @cozysetup.kw, we may process:

  • your Instagram messages to us, and our replies
  • your Instagram account identifier, so we can reply in the same conversation
  • your name and phone number, when you give them to make a booking
  • booking details, such as the date, location and payment choice
  • payment screenshots or other proof of payment that you send us
  • the history of our conversation, so we can continue it later

Why we use it

We use this information only to provide our booking and customer-support services: answering your questions, checking availability, creating and managing your booking, confirming your payment, and contacting you about your booking.

Automated replies

Some of our Instagram replies are written by an automated assistant. The owner of CozySetup.kw can see the conversation and may reply personally at any time.

Who processes it

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. To provide the service, your information is processed by:

  • Meta (Instagram), which delivers messages between you and us
  • OpenAI, which provides the AI service that writes our automated replies, based on the conversation

Your booking details and conversation are stored on systems managed by CozySetup.kw.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete your information, send a Direct Message to @cozysetup.kw on Instagram saying that you want your data deleted. We will delete your messages, contact details, booking details and payment proofs from our systems and confirm by replying to you.

Deleting data from our systems does not delete messages from Instagram itself. You can delete a conversation in the Instagram app.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your information, send a Direct Message to @cozysetup.kw on Instagram.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
